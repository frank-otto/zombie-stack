# Sounds – sources & licenses (Zombie Stack)

All sounds are recorded samples released under **CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No synth beeps, no oscillators. `tools/build.mjs` embeds them as base64 into `js/sounds-src.js` so the game also works via `file://`. Credit is not required for CC0 and is given voluntarily, by source ID only. The graveyard room sound is a generated convolution reverb.

## Music – VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0 1.0)
https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE – trimmed, faded and loudness-matched; the game re-pitches the nearest note. Original menu and stacking grooves (and the rising PERFECT organ chords), sequenced note by note.
